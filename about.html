About Us

RiceQ is a portable, rice-based meal-in-a-cup inspired by the classic Korean gimbap. By reimagining traditional Filipino dishes such as Pork Adobo, Chicken Pastil, Tofu Sisig, Pork Menudo, and Beef Caldereta. RiceQ brings the taste of home into a simple and ready-to-eat form. With its eco-friendly packaging, RiceQ champions sustainability while providing a convenient, affordable, and culturally rooted food option that strengthens its presence in the community.
